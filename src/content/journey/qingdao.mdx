## Ice Breaker

"I'm from a seaside city known for beer and tourism, but I don't drink, don't like seafood, and don't know how to swim" is one of my classic ice breakers.

## The Green Bottle

The most famous thing about [Qingdao](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Qingdao%20City/985933) is probably Tsingtao Beer, the green bottle that you can find at 99 Ranch and most Asian supermarkets.
