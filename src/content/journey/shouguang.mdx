## College Town

My college was called "Weifang University of Science and Technology." Still, it is actually located in [Shouguang](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Shouguang%20City/1001909), a small city I'd probably never have heard of if I hadn't gone to school there, as unknown to most people as Jiaozhou.

## Firsts

However, it holds a lot of my firsts: first time taking a train alone, first time being so far from home, a two-hour drive, which honestly doesn't seem that far now... it takes similar time to drive from Berkeley to SFO in the morning, due to traffic, first time being away from home for four years, first time living like a "real adult," and the first time I dared to tell my family I was dating someone...
