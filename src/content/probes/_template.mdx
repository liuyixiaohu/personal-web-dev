## The question

What you went in wondering, in 1-2 paragraphs. Frame it so a reader who's never thought about this still cares.

## Method, briefly

What data you used, where it came from, what you did to it. Keep this tight — methodology depth goes in a footnote or appendix. Reader trust comes from clarity, not exhaustiveness.

## What I found

The actual insight. This is the heart of the piece — give it room. Charts go here as components, like:

## So what

What this changes for how you'd build / position / measure. Connect the data finding to a PM/PMM decision.

## Caveats

What the analysis can't tell you. Where it could be wrong. Acknowledging these up front is a strength signal, not a weakness one.
